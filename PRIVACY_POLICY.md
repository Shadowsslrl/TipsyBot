TipsyBot ("we", "us", or "our") operates the TipsyBot service. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection.

- Interaction logs

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

We retain user data for as long as necessary to provide services. You may request data deletion by contacting us.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

TipsyBot is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
